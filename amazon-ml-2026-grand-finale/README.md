# Amazon ML Challenge 2026: Grand Finale, Team Androids

Presentation and preparation material for our Grand Finale talk on 7 October 2026.

The task was business entity resolution. Almost 10 million incoming business records from the US, India and France had to be linked to the right business among 1.7 million, or left unlinked. Our final submission scored F0.5 = 0.9913 on the public leaderboard.

Team: Pardheev Krishna Tammineni, Monika Tammineni, Pallam Hemachand.
